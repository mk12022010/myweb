Professor Dr. Md. Kamrul Hasan

UGC Teaching Load Calculator

Course-wise data-entry system based on the supplied Public Universities Teaching Load Calculation Policy (Engineering), 2022.
How this works: Enter one row for each course/activity. Select its semester and teaching-load category. The calculator automatically applies the corresponding policy formula, shows the calculation for every row, aggregates the results by category and semester, and calculates the required number of teachers.

1. Calculation settings

Staffing assumptions

A 20% study-leave allowance is applied in both systems. The adjusted requirement is distributed as Professor : Associate Professor : Assistant Professor : Lecturer = 1 : 2 : 4 : 3.

The policy states 13 hours as the average weekly contact workload for a teacher and 6 hours for a department/institute head.
Semester system: one academic year has two semesters; the higher Odd/Even course load is used. Year System: only four-year undergraduate programmes are included; the four years are combined, the teacher number is calculated, and 65% is retained as the semester-system equivalent.

2. Course/activity data entry

Use one row per course, section, laboratory activity, project/internship/thesis activity, or other applicable teaching activity. For categories that require a historical average, enter the applicable historical average. For MPhil and PhD, this specifically means the previous five-year average.

Policy-based formulas used

This application is a calculator implementation of the formulas in the supplied policy. It does not create a new policy or independently change the policy's formulas.

Source and implementation note

Important: The supplied policy clearly shows the Odd/Even higher-load rule and the teacher-number formula. The Year System + 65% conversion, 20% study-leave allowance, 1:2:4:3 faculty-rank distribution, and corrected MPhil/PhD formulas have been implemented according to the rules specified for this application.

The formulas in this application are based on the supplied Public Universities Teaching Load Calculation Policy (Engineering), 2022. The application preserves the policy's calculation structure rather than introducing an alternative staffing model.

  • Teacher average weekly contact workload: 13 hours.
  • Department/institute head weekly contact workload: 6 hours.
  • Semester System: one academic year has two semesters, and the higher Odd/Even course load is considered.
  • Year System: four undergraduate years are added; the resulting teacher number is multiplied by 65% to obtain the semester-system equivalent.
  • Year System is restricted to undergraduate programmes; postgraduate programmes are not included.
  • UG laboratory: students ÷ 10 × credit hours.
  • UG practical/survey/field/other: students ÷ 15 × credit hours.
  • UG project/internship/thesis: average students over the previous three years × contact hours; the policy image specifies 2 hours, with a separate Architecture/Urban & Regional Planning note of 3 hours.
  • PG theory: average course number over the previous three years × credit hours.
  • PG laboratory: follows the laboratory-load calculation.
  • Master's thesis: historical average degree earners over the previous three years × 3 contact hours.
  • MPhil thesis: previous five-year average MPhil degree earners × 3 contact hours.
  • PhD thesis: previous five-year average PhD degree earners × 4 contact hours.
  • Both systems add a 20% study-leave allowance by default.
  • The adjusted teacher requirement is distributed as Professor : Associate Professor : Assistant Professor : Lecturer = 1 : 2 : 4 : 3.

For categories where the policy requires a previous-year average, the app does not calculate that average automatically unless the user enters the underlying average; this avoids inventing data not supplied by the user.
